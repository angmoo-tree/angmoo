# Community code of conduct

Be respectful, specific, and focused on the work.

- Welcome people regardless of experience or background.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Do not harass, threaten, impersonate, spam, or publish private information.
- Do not use project spaces to coordinate manipulation, targeted abuse, or
  unsafe access to systems or data.
- Report sensitive conduct or security concerns privately.

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content and may restrict
participation when needed to protect contributors and the project. Good-faith
technical disagreement is welcome; abusive conduct is not.
